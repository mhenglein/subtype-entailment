# Q003 — Strict agreement-profile descent

Status: REFUTED
Confidence: written all-length counterexample + reproduced finite checks
Introduced: 2026-09-05
Resolved: 2026-09-08 consolidation of PR #6
Related: Q002, A008, A009, A017, C041, C044, C045

## Original claim

For every accepted word `w` of a `k`-state binary partial DFA with terminal accepting states, if

    word_budget(w) > k-2,

there exists a strictly shorter accepted word `v` with

    A(v) subseteq A(w).

Q003 was stronger than Q002 because repeated strict descent would have terminated at a word of budget at most `k-2`.

## Counterexample

Use the eight-state modular-synchronization instance recorded in
[the resolving proof](../../notes/2026-09-05-modular-synchronization.md).
Its exact witness language is

    { (1^(6t) 0)^2 : t>=1 }.

The unique shortest witness

    w = 11111101111110

has length 14 and exact word budget 7, while `k-2=6`.

If an accepted `v` satisfies `A(v) subseteq A(w)`, then every flag avoided by `w` is also avoided by `v`; hence `v` is a witness of the same instance. Since `w` is the unique shortest witness, no strictly shorter such `v` exists. Thus Q003 is false.

The same research line also gives a separate family showing that no universal constant number of simultaneous equal-state loop deletions suffices: with `r` unary blocks, a profile-dominated shortening can require editing all `r` blocks together. That result is independent of the eight-state refutation.

## Evidence boundary

`python3 experiments/check_modular_sync.py` checks the concrete witness language through the separating length, profile domination of all shorter accepted candidates, exact lower sufficient-budget graphs, and simultaneous-deletion fixtures. The all-length and asymptotic statements rest on written proofs; they are not Lean-checked or independently refereed.

The earlier bounded searches that found profile descent through `k<=7` remain correct finite observations. The first counterexample has eight states and shortest length 14, outside those searches.

## Consequence

Do not use strict profile descent, a constant multi-loop deletion rule, or Q003=>Q002 as proof steps. Local rules such as C045 remain valid under their stated hypotheses, but they cannot be promoted to an unconditional descent theorem.
