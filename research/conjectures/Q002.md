# Q002 — Agreement-profile domination for terminal words

Status: REFUTED

Introduced: 2026-09-05
Resolved: 2026-09-05

## Resolution

C046 gives an eight-state terminal-final binary instance with exact witness
language `{1^(6a) 0 1^(6a) 0 : a>=1}`. Its unique shortest witness is
`11111101111110`, length 14; its minimum sufficient budget is seven, while
`k-2=6`. All 28 lower-budget splits are independently exhausted in the
modular-synchronization regression.

Let w be that witness and G the five flags in the construction. Since G is
disjoint from A(w), any accepted v with A(v) subseteq A(w) also avoids G.
Every such v is therefore in the displayed language and has budget at least
seven. No replacement of ANY length meets the proposed budget.

The construction extends to `B=(r-1)(lcm(p_1,...,p_r)+1)` using
`k=1+sum_i(p_i+1)` states. Taking periods 1,...,m proves a superpolynomial
lower bound `B >= 2^(Omega(sqrt(k)))`. The original small-state census and
finite search outcomes are retained as historical evidence; only their
conjectured extension is refuted. The new proof is written, not Lean-checked.

## Original claim (refuted)

Let M be a k-state partial deterministic automaton over the binary alphabet,
with terminal accepting states. For every boundary-safe accepted word w,
there exists a boundary-safe accepted word v such that

    A(v) subseteq A(w)
    word_budget(v) <= k-2.

A(x) is the full state-pair suffix-agreement profile, including diagonal and
empty-suffix agreements. C044's equivalence between this statement and the
terminal k-2 budget bound remains valid; both equivalent statements are false.

The maximal preserving flags remain useful:

    G_w = (Q x Q) minus A(w).

A low-budget witness for G_w is precisely a profile-dominated replacement.
The reformulation itself is not invalidated by the counterexample.

## Earlier evidence retained

For k=2,3,4, the bounded statement follows from the complete terminal census
C034. Earlier k=5,6,7 searches found no counterexample within their tested
ranges; those were falsification observations, not proofs. Flat terminal
graphs remain decidable by the Presburger skeleton procedure, but flatness
does not imply this budget bound: C046 itself is flat.

Do not use Q002 as the missing global bound. Any general normalization must
admit large arithmetic synchronization; a computable symbolic cover remains
possible but is not supplied by this counterexample.
