# Q002 — Agreement-profile domination for terminal words

Status: REFUTED
Confidence: written all-length counterexample + reproduced finite checks
Introduced: 2026-09-05
Resolved: 2026-09-08 consolidation of PR #6
Related: A008, A009, A017, C034, C041, C044

## Original claim

Let M be a k-state partial deterministic binary automaton with terminal accepting states. For every boundary-safe accepted word `w`, there exists a boundary-safe accepted word `v` such that

    A(v) subseteq A(w)

and

    word_budget(v) <= k-2.

C044 showed this is equivalent to the sharp terminal sufficient-budget bound.

## Counterexample

The eight-state terminal instance in
[modular synchronization](../../notes/2026-09-05-modular-synchronization.md)
has transition table

| state | 0 | 1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0 | undefined | 1 |
| 1 | undefined | 2 |
| 2 | 3 | 1 |
| 3 | undefined | 4 |
| 4 | undefined | 5 |
| 5 | undefined | 6 |
| 6 | 7 | 4 |
| 7 | undefined | undefined |

with sole terminal final `7` and

    G = {(0,4),(0,5),(0,6),(1,3),(2,3)}.

Its exact witness language is

    { (1^(6t) 0)^2 : t>=1 }.

The unique shortest witness is `11111101111110`, length 14, and the exact instance budget is 7, while `k-2=6`.

Let `w` be that shortest witness and `A(w)` its complete agreement profile. Any accepted `v` with `A(v) subseteq A(w)` avoids every flag preserving `w`, hence is itself a witness of this instance. Every witness has budget at least 7. Therefore no profile-dominated replacement with budget at most 6 exists.

The general family with periods `p_1,...,p_r` has exact witness language `(1^N0)^r` for positive common multiples `N`, exact budget `(r-1)(lcm(p_i)+1)`, and gives a `2^(Omega(sqrt(k)))` lower bound along periods `1,...,m`. This disproves every polynomial terminal budget bound, not merely `k-2`.

## Evidence boundary

`python3 experiments/check_modular_sync.py` independently checks the concrete eight-state instance with both witness predicates, exhausts every sufficient-budget split below 7, and tests the parameterized family. The all-parameter conclusion rests on the written proof, not bounded enumeration. The proof is not Lean-checked or independently refereed.

Historical bounded searches supporting Q002 remain valid observations about their tested ranges; they did not establish the conjecture.

## Consequence

Do not use Q002, the sharp `k-2` budget bound, or agreement-profile domination at budget `k-2` as premises. A general decision argument must permit substantially larger arithmetic witnesses or use symbolic representations rather than a polynomial state-count budget.
