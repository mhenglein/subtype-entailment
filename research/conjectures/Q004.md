# Q004 — Computably bounded flagged-source visits

Status: PROVED
Evidence level: ordinary corollary of the Lean-checked terminal decision; C082 now supplies an explicit numerical bound. No new Lean source-visit declaration.

## Exact target

For a binary terminal BorderAvoid instance I with k states, let
`S(I)={p : exists t, (p,t) in G}`. Count visits by **run positions**, including
the initial and terminal positions when their states lie in S(I).

Is there a total computable function R such that every positive k-state
terminal instance has **some** witness with at most R(k) source visits?
There is no proposed polynomial, elementary, or numerical bound here.

## September 20: explicit bound from C082

Put R(0)=0 and R(k)=Fhat(k)+1 for k>=1, where [C082](../claims.md#c082)
defines the corrected recurrence `T_(j+1)=64*k*T_j^(6*T_j^2+2)`.
The checked terminal witness theorem gives a witness of length at most Fhat(k).
Its run has exactly length+1 positions, and source visits are a subset of those
positions. This proves the stated existential Q004 bound. It says nothing
about every witness or about shortestness under a selected cut rule.

[Proof/readback and fresh checks](../../notes/2026-09-20-post-c081-quantitative-integration.md).
The numerical witness theorem is Lean checked; the source-position corollary
here is ordinary mathematics. The older classification proof below remains
valid and does not depend on the new formula. No efficient bound is claimed.

## September 19: resolved by complete terminal classification

The exact terminal theorem is now
`RankQueryDecision.terminalWitnessCheck_correct (I : Instance k)
(hT : TerminalFinals I) : terminalWitnessCheck I = true ↔ ∃ w, Witness I w`.
It requires no computable witness-bound or clause-weight premise. C081 records
its completed subtype composition, but that composition is not needed for
this source-visit corollary.

Define a total computable R as follows. Put R(0)=0; `Instance 0` has no initial
state. For k>=1 enumerate all labelled `Fin k` tables: the initial state,
each partial binary transition, final and Bad bits, and each ordered flag bit.
All fields are finite, so this list is effective and exhaustive. Test terminal
finals by checking every final state's two transitions. Classify each retained
instance with the total terminal Boolean. For each YES instance, enumerate
finite binary words by increasing length until the decidable original Witness
predicate succeeds. Correctness of the Boolean guarantees termination of each
positive search; it is never started on a NO instance. Take the maximum of
`|w|+1` over the finitely many resulting witnesses, or 0 for an empty family.
Every run on w has exactly `|w|+1` positions, so its source visits obey this
bound. Relabelling an arbitrary k-state carrier to `Fin k` preserves runs,
flags and source positions; the formal theorem already uses that carrier.
Thus R is total, computable and satisfies exactly the existential Q004 target.

This is an ordinary proof, independently reviewed in the C081 completion
record, not a formalized R implementation. It gives no efficient algorithm,
closed formula or claim about C080's proposed F. Existing lower bounds and
failed local-deletion examples remain valid. Prior OPEN statements below
describe their earlier checkpoints and are superseded only by this argument.
[Terminal theorem and validation](../../notes/2026-09-19-terminal-rank-decision.md),
[completion record and review](../../notes/2026-09-19-entailment-decision.md),
[C081](../claims.md#c081).

## Earlier motivation: why this would suffice

C068 decides witness existence with a supplied finite source-visit bound via
existential word equations and regular constraints. It permits arbitrary
unbounded unflagged data paths. Conversely, a general terminal decider would
supply such a function by classifying the finitely many labelled k-state
instances and then enumerating a witness for each positive one. At that earlier checkpoint this was only an
equivalence with decidability. The checked terminal decider now makes it the
proof of existence given above.

C067 supplies one-visit normalization only when all flags leave a single state.
A source-acyclic productive graph gives the elementary bound |S(I)|. Neither
fact implies a bound on interactions between recurrent source states.

## September 12: recovered quantitative and symbolic evidence

existing C046 modular family. With prime periods all flags are genuinely live,
and the counts grow as `exp((1+o(1))*sqrt(k*log(k)))` along that family.
This rules out polynomial choices of R, not this computable-bound conjecture.
The constructed family itself is flat and has an exact arithmetic decision.

input. No such bound is proved, and it is not the same quantity as total visits.

## September 13: the precise power-scheme cost can also be exponential

C076 gives a family with exact minimum C075 scheme cost

## September 13: a quadratic bound for a certified target-anchored class

C077 proves a complete native decision for complete target columns by reducing
all comparisons to uniqueness of the LAST target suffix. Productive target-free
tail cycles also supply a short witness for arbitrary flags. C078 extends the
complete-column decision to a finite-graph-certified sparse class, without
increasing state count or word length. Positive certified k-state slices have
a witness of length at most `k^2+k-2`, hence at most `k^2+k-1` source positions.
These are scoped bounds, not a polynomial R for all instances.

The known positive (2,3) modular instance and the known six-state NO marking
barrier both remain UNKNOWN under this certificate. Completing columns without
certification can erase an existing witness. The outstanding obligation is an
existence-preserving treatment of distinct legal target suffixes retaining one
shared word, not a further numerical cutoff. [Proof, native code and negative
certificate replay](../TARGET_ANCHOR.md). Written proof + reproducible checks;
independent mathematical review pending, not Lean. Q004 stayed OPEN at this checkpoint.

## September 13: source-sensitive target-return elimination

C079 gives a complete native decision when every incoming target column is
closed under productive synchronized returns to its ORIGINAL target label.
Legal repeated suffixes are retained. Exact Km+1-state fixed-tail filters and
at most r original-column eliminations give witness length at most
`2*k^(2^r)`, hence at most one more source position, for the certified normalized
class. Dynamic checks after filtering safely decide further examples.

This does not supply R for arbitrary inputs. The existing modular word
111101111110 passes every last-per-target check but has a forbidden earlier
comparison; omitting return closure is unsound. Both the modular positive and
marking-barrier negative input remain UNKNOWN here. Target-state copies cannot
silently replace original target groups. [Proof, scope and complete finite
certificate replay](../TARGET_RETURN.md). Written proof plus reproduction,
independent mathematical review pending, not Lean. Q004 remained OPEN at this checkpoint.

## September 15–18: a candidate witness-length bound would settle this question

[C080](../claims.md#c080) proposes, as a CANDIDATE that is not yet reviewed by a
human referee and is Lean-checked only in its first two stages, an explicit
computable witness-length bound `F(k)` for every positive binary terminal
instance. Source visits are run positions, so `F(k)+1` would bound them and
`R=F+1` would answer this question positively. The candidate does not reason
about source visits at all: it eliminates unknown reference words by finite
query preimages over first-return prefix graphs. Until its stage-4
promotion-preimage lemma is independently confirmed, Q004 remained OPEN at that checkpoint; do not
cite `F(k)` as an established `R`.
