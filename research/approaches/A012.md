### 2026-09-08 — Three-case symbolic certificate for the five-state all-budget obstruction

Recorded by ChatGPT from main `99133037a60f65c8e0fab5ed9299524fbd95043e`, prompted by issue #15.

The five-state PR #9 NO instance has accepted words exactly
`1^n 0 1^m 0`, `n>=0,m>=1`. A finite three-branch certificate schema now
selects an actual forbidden suffix comparison for every accepted candidate:

- `n<m`: cuts `(0,m+1)` witness flag `(0,3)`;
- `n=m`: cuts `(0,n+1)` witness flag `(0,2)`;
- `n>m`: cuts `(n-m,n+1)` witness flag `(1,2)`.

Each branch has a written all-length proof that the target suffix is a prefix
of the source suffix and that the two run states are the stated flag pair.
This gives an explicit finite symbolic NO certificate for the instance even
though every finite `Necessary(L,D)` language is nonempty. The important
structural distinction is that the violating cut is allowed to depend on the
candidate parameters.

`python3 experiments/check_five_state_no_certificate.py` independently
reimplements the automaton and direct suffix predicate. The recorded regression
checks 1,056 parameter pairs, all 8,191 binary words through length 12 (55
accepted, zero witnesses), and 4,225 special-family Necessary identities.
The bounded tests support the implementation; the universal result rests on
the written three-case proof.

[Proof and limitations](../../notes/2026-09-08-five-state-no-certificate.md),
[checker](../../experiments/check_five_state_no_certificate.py),
[result](../../results/five-state-no-certificate.json).

This is the first concrete A012 certificate attempt, but it does not prove a
complete calculus for arbitrary NO instances. The next discriminating target
is the seven-state different-gap obstruction: seek a finite candidate
partition plus a mechanically checkable symbolic violating-cut selector.
