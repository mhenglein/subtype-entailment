### 2026-09-04 — Shortest census and critical occurrences

Owner: Astra, with independent word checks by `adversarial_budget` and
structure analysis by `context_cut`.

The complete two-state census is decided: 2,682 instances have a shortest
witness of length zero or one; the other 18,054 have an empty zero-budget
necessary graph. All nonempty instances have budget zero. The general
at-most-two-state statement now has a checked binary Lean proof.

A separate 120-attempt generator preserves a candidate word while adding
flags that eliminate shorter witnesses. Six retained 7–8-state instances
have exact budgets `5,4,3,2,2,2` and shortest lengths up to 12. These are
selected examples, not a census of larger automata.

The written structure note extracts a critical earlier occurrence from every
final, suffix-matching proper prefix of a shortest witness. Its positions
remain unbounded. A three-state example proves that deleting a repeated-state
loop can create a forbidden proper border. See [C019](../claims.md#c019),
[E014](../../experiments/README.md#e014), and the
[search note](../../notes/2026-09-04-shortest-witnesses.md).

Outcome: restricted decisions and precise obstructions; the global bound
remains open. Trim irrelevant states before interpreting state-count growth.

### 2026-09-04 - Exact three-state bound and actual terminal obstruction

Recorded by: ChatGPT. C022 gives a written complete three-state classification:
witness existence is equivalent to nonempty Necessary(1,1), and every positive
instance has a witness of length at most six and budget at most two. Both
bounds are sharp. All 65,536 normalized binary cases were checked, including
independent complete negative-graph exhaustion. The proof is not Lean-checked.

C028 subsequently supplies an actual eight-variable subtype example whose
shortest separating path 1101 needs a pre-final loop. The same-state deletion
1101 -> 111 is unsound. All its separating paths are terminal, exposing the
remaining branch after the infinite-continuation decision C029.

See [C021-C029 and commands](../CONTINUATION.md), the
[three-state proof](../../notes/2026-09-04-three-state.md), and the
[denial-aware proof](../../notes/2026-09-04-denials-and-continuations.md).
The general witness bound remains open; the new infinite-continuation bound
has an explicit category restriction.

### 2026-09-05 - Complete four-state terminal census

Recorded by: ChatGPT. C034/E024 exhaust all 65,536 normalized binary
terminal-final instances: 45,430 YES, 20,106 NO, no unresolved cases or
resource-cap exits. A written normalization covers every terminal-final
instance with at most four states. Every negative has a complete necessary-
automaton emptiness check; every positive passes both word predicates.
Witness length at most four and budget at most two are sharp for this class.
This is a finite computational classification using audited Python graphs,
not a Lean theorem or a bound for larger state counts. The earlier general
three-state result remains separate.

See [C030-C034 / E021-E024](../TERMINAL.md), the
