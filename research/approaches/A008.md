### 2026-09-04 — Shortest census and critical occurrences

Owner: Astra, with independent word checks by `adversarial_budget` and
structure analysis by `context_cut`.

The complete two-state census is decided: 2,682 instances have a shortest
witness of length zero or one; the other 18,054 have an empty zero-budget
necessary graph. All nonempty instances have budget zero. The general
at-most-two-state statement now has a checked binary Lean proof.

A separate 120-attempt generator preserves a candidate word while adding
flags that eliminate shorter witnesses. Six retained 7–8-state instances
have exact budgets `5,4,3,2,2,2` and shortest lengths up to 12. These are
selected examples, not a census of larger automata.

The written structure note extracts a critical earlier occurrence from every
final, suffix-matching proper prefix of a shortest witness. Its positions
remain unbounded. A three-state example proves that deleting a repeated-state
loop can create a forbidden proper border. See [C019](../claims.md#c019),
[E014](../../experiments/README.md#e014), and the
[search note](../../notes/2026-09-04-shortest-witnesses.md).

Outcome: restricted decisions and precise obstructions; the global bound
remains open. Trim irrelevant states before interpreting state-count growth.
