# A016: Implement and audit fixed-skeleton checkability

Current status, owner, and next step: [research tracker](../README.md). Metadata lives in [approaches.json](../approaches.json).

## Question and scope

Turn the memo's Proposition 7.5 into a checked procedure for fixed words s0, y1, s1, ..., yd, sd, with each yi nonempty and nonnegative repetition counts free. Every Fin, Bad, and ordered G condition must be retained.

## Attempt history

### 2026-09-04 — Research-plan proposal

Recorded by Astra from the ten-angle outline. This is an audit and implementation proposal for a claim already present in the memo. No translation or solver has been implemented here.

Start with s u^k t. Derive an effective threshold and period for valid k, then handle two blocks and cross-block overlaps. Compare the arithmetic translation with the direct witness predicate on finite exponent ranges. Record a proof for the unbounded exponent cases.

Next, investigate partial DFAs whose recurrent strongly connected components are simple directed cycles. Enumerate the finite path skeletons and reduce each to the checked fixed-skeleton procedure.

## Result and limits

A successful translation gives a solver for the named restricted language family. A solution to general BorderAvoid would also need an effective theorem that some witness has a bounded skeleton; [A017](A017.md) investigates that missing step. Bounded exponent tests alone do not establish the translation.
