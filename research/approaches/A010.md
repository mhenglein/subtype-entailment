# A010: Build the exact top-free constraint-to-automata pipeline

Current status, owner, and next step: [research tracker](../README.md). Metadata lives in [approaches.json](../approaches.json).

## Question and scope

Normalized top-free subtype constraints, including bottom denials and unconditional product facts.

## Attempt history

Recorded by: Astra, from the September 4 review and committed evidence.

### 2026-09-04 — Engineering and proof

Only a hand-analyzed one-constraint example has been reproduced. The general bridge and model checker have not been built.

### 2026-09-04 — Supplied-path closure and exact regular checks

Owner: Astra (`bridge_foundation`), with independent review by `bridge_audit`.

Implemented all four normalized clause forms, finite-seed P-automaton
saturation, regular-language inclusion and prefix-closure checks, forced-root
satisfiability, and canonical countermodels for a supplied path. The audit
found no orientation or empty-stack error. It identified a mixed-ID sorting
issue in the diagnostic helper; the fix and regression are included.

`python3 experiments/check_topfree_bridge.py` passes 3,577 bounded diagnostic
comparisons and targeted edge cases. Candidate failures use `REJECTED_PATH`,
separate from `UNSAT`. The runtime model checks use complete finite automata
operations, while the independent direct closure is explicitly bounded.

This establishes an audited implementation stage, not a formalized all-input
theorem or full W-to-summary-class pipeline. General input normalization,
summary classes, and global candidate search remain. See
[C015](../claims.md#c015), [E009](../../experiments/README.md#e009), and the
[foundation note](../../notes/2026-09-04-bridge-foundation.md).

### 2026-09-04 — Actual summary classes and preserved S/F conditions

Owner: Astra (`bridge_foundation`), independently audited by `bridge_audit`.

Built paired exact Pop summaries from one W and its reversal, deterministic
summary classes, intersection flags, forced-root endpoint exclusion F, and
every-prefix denial exclusion S. Empty summary subsets and query variables
absent from constraints remain supported.

The audit found two API errors: generator inputs lost push rules on a second
pass, and unsatisfiable systems were not rejected before applying the
SAT-conditional reduction. Both now have fixes and regressions. Independent
bounded oracles report UNKNOWN when caps prune unexplored successors.

The targeted check passes 762 word comparisons. The random audit passes
28,956 summary/canonical comparisons, 68,839 known Pop comparisons, and
10,675 known denial comparisons. Its unresolved direct searches remain
explicitly UNKNOWN. See [C017](../claims.md#c017),
[E013](../../experiments/README.md#e013), and the
[audit note](../../notes/2026-09-04-summary-bridge-audit.md).

The bridge is ready to generate actual subtype-derived instances. General
input normalization, all-input Lean verification, and complete witness search
remain open.
