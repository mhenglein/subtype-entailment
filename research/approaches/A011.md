### 2026-09-05 - Exact terminal-final converse

Recorded by: ChatGPT. C030 gives an always-satisfiable normalized subtype
realization of every k-state binary terminal-final annotated automaton on
2k+1 variables. Arbitrary flags, Bad states, and initial states are retained.
The separating word language and every fixed-budget predicate are preserved
without coding or an added letter. C031 combines this converse with C029 and
the audited forward bridge to equate terminal-final decidability with the
normalized subtype problem. This is not the full arbitrary-BorderAvoid
converse, nor a complexity equivalence. Proofs are written, not Lean-checked.

E021 checks 166 instances, 10,458 words, 83,664 budget comparisons, and 2,985
exact regular countermodels. C032/E022 supplies an actual nine-variable
nonregular terminal separating language. See [claims and commands](../TERMINAL.md)
and the [complete construction proof](../../notes/2026-09-05-terminal-realization.md).

### 2026-09-05 — Direct regular-copy reduction without ambient top

Recorded by ChatGPT on `research/terminal-copy-synchronization`, base `2c6b4aac`.
The target was to turn C030's terminal converse into a fragment-specific
hardness construction, avoiding the ambient-top gap in the older literature
comparison.

C047 constructs a terminal automaton whose witnesses are exactly `(u#)^r`
for u accepted by every member of a supplied list of binary DFAs. Remembering
one predecessor letter, or a fresh entry marker, lets a longest-common-suffix
argument use forbidden state pairs to force whole-block equality. C048 codes
the ternary intermediate automaton back into binary, placing flags only at
code-boundary states and preserving witness languages exactly.

C049 composes these polynomial constructions with C030 to reduce binary DFA
intersection nonemptiness to subtype non-entailment on satisfiable normalized
bottom/product systems. It gives a written PSPACE-hardness proof for the exact
top-free semantic fragment; complement closure also gives entailment hardness.
The source intersection theorem was checked in a primary paper. E029 compares
126 input tuples with independent product decisions and checks encoded words
and exact regular subtype models.

Outcome: written reduction with reproducible implementation checks, pending
independent mathematical review and not Lean-checked. Existing C030/forward
bridge dependencies retain their recorded statuses. No unrestricted decider,
upper bound, arbitrary nonterminal converse, or literature priority is claimed.
The constructed subclass is exactly decidable by product reachability when its
component DFAs are supplied.

Next: independently audit the longest-common-suffix argument, binary alignment,
and C030 composition; use synchronized-block structure in the symbolic upper-
bound work without confusing a lower bound with a decision procedure.
