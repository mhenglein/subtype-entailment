# A017: Bound essential repeated blocks

Current status, owner, and next step: [research tracker](../README.md). Metadata lives in [approaches.json](../approaches.json).

## Question and scope

Does every successful BorderAvoid instance have a witness describable by a fixed repetition skeleton whose size is effectively bounded by the instance encoding?

### 2026-09-08 - Single-source junction and shape lemmas; exact five-state census

Recorded by Claude. Base main: `9913303`. Branch
`claude/subtail-repo-strategy-hiom63`. Continues the same-day strategy entry.

flags leave the start state), is the shortest witness computably bounded?

**Method and result.** Written proofs: a junction lemma classifying every
T-period created by a loop deletion as "long border spanning the junction"
or "small period with the deleted word fully periodic"; end loops always
give the second type; two lemmas show that whenever the periodic structure
has primitive root of length at most `n`, whole-period deletion produces a
shorter witness, so `N <= n^2 + 6n`. Theorem: a shortest single-source
witness longer than `n^2 + 6n` is a prefix of `z0^omega` (primitive `z0`,
`|z0| > n`, at most `n+2` periods) followed by a defect and a tail shorter
than `n`, with a T-state at a multiple of `|z0|`; hence `N < (n+2)|z0| + n`.

instances decided, longest shortest witness 4 and 6.
