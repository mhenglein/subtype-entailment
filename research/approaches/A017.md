# A017: Bound essential repeated blocks

Current status, owner, and next step: [research tracker](../README.md). Metadata lives in [approaches.json](../approaches.json).

## Question and scope

Does every successful BorderAvoid instance have a witness describable by a fixed repetition skeleton whose size is effectively bounded by the instance encoding?

## Attempt history

### 2026-09-04 — Research-plan proposal

Recorded by Astra from the ten-angle outline. No decomposition theorem or block-deletion experiment has been attempted here.

First study words x u^k y, then two interacting repeated blocks. Record the transition effect of each block and all new borders crossing a deletion boundary. Test proposed deletion hypotheses against the independent witness predicate and minimize failures.

Investigate the transition monoid and [factorization forests](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.5130) as ways to organize repeated behavior. The theorem supplies bounded-height decompositions for finite semigroups. It does not itself bound the number of relevant blocks or preserve our endpoint flags.

### 2026-09-04 — Exact loop-deletion obligation

Owner: Astra (`context_cut`).

An interior deletion between equal DFA states preserves the run and endpoint.
To preserve witnesses, every newly agreeing suffix pair that crosses the
join must lift to an agreeing pair before deletion. A written sufficient
condition makes this explicit. The three-state `001 -> 00` example shows
why repeated states alone fail.

This is a precise local proof obligation, not a finite skeleton theorem.
It supplies no bound on the number of essential blocks. See
[C019](../claims.md#c019) and the
[structure note](../../notes/2026-09-04-minimal-witness-structure.md).

## Result and limits

Success requires an effective normalization theorem plus correct preservation of every witness obligation. Combining such a theorem with a checked implementation of [A016](A016.md) could give a decision algorithm. Equality of transition effects alone is insufficient because deletion can create new borders.
