### 2026-09-04 — Seeded adversarial search

Owner: Astra (`adversarial_budget`), with integration review by Astra.

Ran 1,200 generated abstract binary instances with 2–8 states, seed
`2026090409`, budgets through 8, a 50,000-state cap per split, and a direct
word scan through length 12. Matching no-flag instances provide baselines.
Every EXACT row exhausts all lower budget splits and returns a witness that
passes both direct predicates.

The flagged search returned 741 EXACT, 414 NO_THROUGH, and 45 UNKNOWN rows.
The largest exact random budget was 1; five rows increased the matching
no-flag budget from 0 to 1. No instance with certified `B>|K|` was found.
The singleton calibrations reproduce the existing linear family through
`n=12`, with `B=11` on 13 states.

A separate stored-row replay checks direct sufficient membership and exact
budget exhaustion. Three of the five `+1` increases survive encoding diagonal
flags as Bad states and start-to-final flags as endpoint exclusions. Two are
explained by those simple exclusions. The replay has its own command and JSON
section; it does not regenerate the census.

Outcome: inconclusive on stronger growth. Bounded failure to find such a
family is not evidence of a general upper bound. Next bias generation toward
bordered accepted words, preserve a known witness while mutating flags, and
separate trivial state/endpoint exclusions from flag effects on proper
borders. See [E011](../../experiments/README.md#e011) and the
[detailed note](../../notes/2026-09-04-budget-search.md).

### 2026-09-04 — Full boundary normalization and genuine proper-border growth

Owner: Astra, with independent boundary review by `lean_plan_review` and
reduced-example review by `context_cut`.

Fixing each final state turns every empty/full-border constraint into an
exact forbidden-state set. All five earlier E011 increases disappear under
this complete normalization. The earlier partial-normalization numbers are
preserved above; they did not isolate proper borders.

The new targeted search does isolate proper-border growth: its strongest
raw instance has budget five versus full-boundary budget one. Restricting it
to the six states visited by the sufficient witness preserves budget five.
Independent finite search confirms all lower budgets empty. This refutes the
universal extension of the regular-subclass bound `B<=max(0,|K|-2)`.

See [C016](../claims.md#c016), [C020](../claims.md#c020),
[E012](../../experiments/README.md#e012), and
[E015](../../experiments/README.md#e015). The six-state example does not refute
`B<=|K|`, establish a growth family, or provide a subtype realization.

Recorded by: ChatGPT. C021 establishes the all-parameter family with exact

### 2026-09-05 — Terminal synchronization forces super-polynomial budgets

Recorded by ChatGPT on `research/terminal-copy-synchronization`, base `2c6b4aac`.
C046 constructs a binary flat terminal instance for every positive period tuple
p_1,...,p_r, r>=2. It has k=1+r+sum p_i states, exact witness language
`{(1^a0)^r : a>0 and lcm(p_i) divides a}`, and exact budget
`(r-1)(lcm(p_i)+1)`. Disjoint unary cycles enforce divisibility while flags
enforce equality of the block lengths.

The (2,3) instance has k=8 and B=7, refuting terminal Q002/Q003. Periods
1 through t give k=Theta(t^2) and B>=2^(t-1) for t>=3, so no polynomial
terminal-budget bound is possible. Unlike the earlier nonterminal abstract
family, C030 realizes these examples as genuine subtype instances while
preserving words and budgets. E029 checks 100 parameter tuples and exhausts
all lower splits for seven named tuples. Larger rows are formula evaluations,
not silently reported exhaustive searches.

Outcome: written exact family and super-polynomial lower bound, with
reproduction; pending independent review and not Lean-checked. No computable
bound is refuted. The graphs are flat and their skeletons have few unary loops,
so large exponents do not contradict the A016 arithmetic or Q001 skeleton route.

Next: use the LCM family and C047 regular-copy construction as mandatory
adversarial tests for candidate upper bounds and symbolic normalizations.
[Proof](../../notes/2026-09-05-terminal-copy-synchronization.md),
[C046](../claims.md#c046), [E029](../../experiments/README.md#e029).
