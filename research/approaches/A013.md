### 2026-09-05 - Terminal realizability limits the structural route

Recorded by: ChatGPT. C030 realizes every terminal-final abstract flag pattern
with the actual shared B/A/D construction. Thus shared structure alone cannot
exclude hard terminal instances. C031 identifies terminal-final BorderAvoid

not an existence decision. C033 supplies that decision for flat terminal
