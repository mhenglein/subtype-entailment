# A015: Strengthen truncation using occurrence context

Current status, owner, and next step: [research tracker](../README.md). Metadata lives in [approaches.json](../approaches.json).

## Question and scope

Can the incoming-flag comparison in C009 be restricted to states or positions relevant to the proposed cut, while preserving every witness condition?

## Attempt history

### 2026-09-04 — Research-plan proposal

Recorded by Astra from the ten-angle outline. No new proof or search has run for this refinement.

First compare incoming flags only at states visited in the retained prefix. Then investigate finer restrictions based on earlier occurrences of each suffix. State each candidate rule separately, including the empty suffix, diagonal pairs, accepting endpoint, and defined-run requirements.

### 2026-09-04 — Context refinements proved and checked

Owner: Astra (`context_cut`); Lean integration and proof review by Astra.

Proved that C009's all-state flag inclusion can be restricted successively
to visited states, states before each suffix, and states at exact earlier
occurrences of that suffix. The proof handles diagonal flags and the empty
suffix separately. It makes no claim that these are minimal possible contexts.

The two-state example `0010 -> 00` passes the visited-state condition while
C009 fails. The example `1011 -> 101` passes the exact-occurrence condition
while the visited-state and earlier-position conditions fail.

`python3 experiments/check_context_cuts.py` exhausts 20,736 two-state
instances through word length 6 and checks 120 larger random instances.
It reports no failed implications. The witness oracle is cross-checked with
the independent reversed-border formulation. The binary visited-state lemma
and its strict example also compile in Lean.

See [C013](../claims.md#c013), [E010](../../experiments/README.md#e010), and
the [proof and limits](../../notes/2026-09-04-context-cut.md). The next useful
step is to characterize shortest witnesses that resist these cuts. The
exact-occurrence criterion alone largely restates local witness validity;
it does not supply a finite summary or bound.

## Result and limits

Success is a proved stronger sufficient cut, or a minimized counterexample to a named relaxation. No finite bound follows merely from strengthening a cut rule.
