## Construction and exact obligations

1. Existing InfiniteCycle supplies a safe nonempty cycle of length at most k,
   based at the end of an original witness with empty incoming column. It does
   not bound the prefix. InfiniteDecision will enumerate and check exactly
   these finite cycle candidates.
2. PeriodicRun will define the literal stream consisting of a supplied finite
   word followed by the repeated cycle. Its PrefixGood predicate forbids
   ordered flagged equal infinite suffixes at old cuts. A safe prefix and safe
   cycle yield a safe trace. Under absorption and finality, PrefixGood then
   supplies InfWitness via InfiniteWitness. Conversely every original witness
   supplies PrefixGood for any continuation. This connection is open until the
   exact declarations compile and receive separate source readback.
3. PeriodicMonitor will decide the safe PrefixGood prefixes using the exact
   reverse phase/bucket invariant from the route note. Its graph has a finite
   explicitly enumerated state type, successor list, cycle-base seed and
   initial-control accepting condition. Rotation equality must preserve
   nonprimitive periods. First mismatch cannot repair already rejected pairs.
   The full accepted-path language invariant is the central remaining gap.
4. FiniteSearch supplies generic total graph saturation from a complete state
   list. The proof shows that each nonfixed round grows the duplicate-free
   visited list, so saturation is exact after the number of listed states.
   The runtime stops early at a fixed point. This bounds a finite graph search,
   not the length of an original witness. Zero-edge paths are included.
5. Compose soundness and completeness with finite candidate enumeration. The
   finished theorem must have only the explicit absorption hypothesis, without
   monitor, cycle-trace, reachability, witness-bound or reduction assumptions.
